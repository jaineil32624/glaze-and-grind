What you do

A blend of restaurant rush and tycoon strategy.

Survive the rush

Customers flood in at 7am, noon and after work. Keep the queue moving or watch them walk out.

Manage a living crew

Staff pick tasks on their own, get stressed, make mistakes and level up. Set their focus, send them on breaks, give raises.

Pull the perfect batch

Tap the fryer and stop the needle in the green zone for a PERFECT batch — or burn it.

Run the business

Restock 13 ingredients, upgrade 7 stations, read the daily ledger and keep the lights on.

Delight critics & influencers

A happy influencer can go viral. A food critic's review counts five times.

Build an empire

Open the Mall, Highway, Airport, City Center and Mega Flagship stores and climb from Trainee to CEO.
